Bundled anime ID mapping

Nuvio bundles anime-list-mini.json from Fribb/anime-lists, built from
anime-offline-database (cedya77, continuing the work of manami-project) and
Anime-Lists/anime-lists. The existing application attribution identifies the
database under the Open Database License 1.0 and its contents under the
Database Contents License 1.0. It identifies the bundled mapping as a
derivative database available under the same license. Nuvio is not affiliated
with or endorsed by these projects.

Full, unchanged license texts are included here as ODBL-1.0.txt and DBCL-1.0.txt.
Their authoritative URLs, hashes, the exact bundled JSON identity, and unresolved
upstream revision/generation evidence are recorded in PROVENANCE.json. The
JSON itself remains at the desktop resource root as anime-list-mini.json; it is
not encrypted or changed by this notice addition. This notice does not claim
that the missing provenance or recipient-source-delivery work is complete.
